Requirement 1 — Financial ratios & dashboard after login
----------------------------------------------------------
• All three users land on their role home page; dashboard.js injects the same
  “Financial Ratios Dashboard” with live GET /reports/ratios.
• Say on camera: “Once authenticated, everyone sees ratio cards fed from the chart
  of accounts.”


Requirement 2 — Menus on the landing page by role
-------------------------------------------------
TOP BAR (app shell — all logged-in roles):
  Home | Chart of Accounts | Event log | Journal | Log out

QUICK NAV CARDS (injected on the home page):
  • regularBrandon (accountant): Chart of Accounts, Journal, Event log  (3 cards)
  • managerBrandon:               same 3 cards
  • adminBrandon:                 those 3 + “Users & reports” + “Add User” → admin-home.html / admin-add-user.html  (5 cards)

Say: “Admins get Users & reports and Add User shortcuts; managers and accountants see
the operational links but not user admin.”


Requirement 3 — Important messages (e.g. pending journals)
------------------------------------------------------------
With video_demo_brandon_seed.sql applied:
  • Banner shows 2 pending journal entries (created by regularBrandon).
  • Wording:
      - adminBrandon & managerBrandon: “…awaiting your approval” + Review link
      - regularBrandon: “…pending approval” + View entries link
  • adminBrandon may also see expired-password alerts if /admin/users/expired-passwords returns users.

Say: “This section surfaces action items without leaving the home page.”


Requirement 4 — Green / yellow / red ratio coding
-------------------------------------------------
• Legend on the dashboard explains Good / Borderline / Needs attention / No data.
• With the demo seed, expect a MIX of colors (e.g. current ratio in yellow range,
  several green metrics, inventory turnover often borderline or red).
• Optional narration: “Thresholds follow typical classroom ranges (liquidity,
  leverage, margins, turnover).”


Requirement 5 — Look and feel consistent
----------------------------------------
• Same style.css, has-app-shell top bar, add-user-section styling for the ratio block.
• Logo / typography / buttons match Chart of Accounts and other pages.

Say: “The home dashboard reuses the same shell and section styles as the rest of the app.”
